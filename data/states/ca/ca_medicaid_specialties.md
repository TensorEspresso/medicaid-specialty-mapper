## Key Notes

- **Time OR distance** — MCPs may use either standard to demonstrate compliance
- **Telehealth** — permitted where necessary for time/distance compliance, EXCEPT for General Surgery, Orthopedic Surgery, Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation, and Hospitals (APL 23-001)
- **Core specialists** — time/distance standards apply only to core specialists listed in Table 2; timely access applies to ALL specialists
- **HIV/AIDS specialists** — requires additional qualifications per DHCS HIV/AIDS qualification list; flagged with "Y" in 274 file
- **Mental health (non-psychiatry)** — includes psychologists, LCSWs, LMFTs, and interns. Psychiatry is a separate core specialist
- **Mandatory providers** — FQHCs, RHCs, FBCs, CNMs, LMs, IHFs must be included where available (APL 19-002)
- **Taxonomy crosswalk** — available publicly via ArcGIS at `networks-gis.dhcs.ca.gov` (863 records, April 2023). Also available via email request to `DHCS-PMU@dhcs.ca.gov` or `MCQMDNAU@dhcs.ca.gov`
