## Florida Core Specialists

### Primary Care & Specialists
- Primary Care Providers
- Medical Specialties (Allergy, Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, General Surgery, Infectious Diseases, Internal Medicine, Nephrology, Neurology, Neurosurgery, OB/GYN, Oncology, Ophthalmology, Optometry, Orthopedic Surgery, Otolaryngology, Pediatrics, Pharmacy, Podiatry, Pulmonology, Rheumatology, Urology)
- Therapy (Occupational, Speech, Physical, Respiratory — including Pediatric variants)

### Dental & Behavioral Health
- General Dentist, Pediatric Dentist, Endodontist, Orthodontist, Oral Surgeon
- LPHA, Child Psychiatrist, Adult Psychiatrist, BCBA, BCaBA, RBT, Substance Abuse Detox Units

### Facility & Organization
- 24/7 Emergency Service Facility, DME/HME, Home Health Agency, Hospice, Acute Care Hospitals, Psychiatric Community Hospitals, Methadone Treatment Programs

## Key Notes

- **Pediatric Variants**: Several specialties (Cardiology, Endocrinology, Nephrology, Neurology) have separate Pediatric designations with higher ratios and longer access times.
- **Behavioral Health Weighting**: BCaBA counts as 0.5 Lead Analyst (BCBA) for ratio purposes.
- **Dental Referrals**: Periodontists and Prosthodontists are available on a referral basis rather than direct network enrollment.
- **Pharmacy**: 24-hour pharmacies are measured by geographic access only (no provider ratio).

## Data Gaps

1. **NUCC Mapping**: Licensed Practitioners of the Healing Arts (LPHA) is a Florida-specific designation with no known NUCC equivalent.
