**Sources:**

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-05

## Overview

NY Medicaid managed care uses a comprehensive provider network framework defined by the Department of Health (DOH) through the Provider Network Data System (PNDS). Provider requirements vary by program type (Medicaid, CHP, HARP, HIV SNP, MAP, MLTC, PACE, FIDA) and by county (urban vs. rural classification).

**Behavioral Health Network Requirements:**
- Outpatient Mental Health Clinics: Higher of 50% of all licensed clinics or minimum of 2 per county (urban) / per region (rural)
- OMH State-Operated Clinics: All in county / All in region
- PROS/IPRT/Continuing Day Treatment: Higher of 50% of total sites or minimum of 2
- Assertive Community Treatment (ACT): 2 per county (urban) / 2 per region (rural)
- Partial Hospitalization: 2 per county (urban) / 2 per region (rural)
- Inpatient Psychiatric Services: 2 per county (urban) / 2 per region (rural)
- CPEP and 9.39 ERs: 2 of each service type per county/region (as available)
- OASAS Inpatient Chemical Dependency: 2 per county (urban) / 2 per region (rural)
- Medically Managed Detox: 2 per county (urban) / 2 per region (rural)
- Outpatient Chemical Dependency: 50% of all licensed clinics or at least 2 per county
- Opioid Treatment Programs: All programs in county (NYC: all in NYC) / All programs in region
- Residential Substance Abuse Treatment: 2 per county (urban) / 2 per region (rural)
- Buprenorphine Prescribers: All authorized prescribers in service area

## Facility Requirements

### Hospitals

| County Group | Minimum Requirement |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Bronx, NYC, Queens, Kings, Nassau, Suffolk, Westchester, Erie | 3 per county |
| Albany, Chautauqua, Monroe, Niagara, Oneida, Onondaga, Orange, Richmond, St. Lawrence | 2 per county |
| All Other Counties | 1 per county (where available) |

**Exceptions:** No hospitals in Greene, Hamilton, Seneca, Tioga, Washington counties. If no hospital within 30 min/30 miles, MCO must include the next closest site.

### Pharmacy

MCO must contract with 24-hour pharmacies ensuring access within 30 minutes from enrollee's residence. If none within 30 min, must include the closest site.

## Home and Community Based Services (HCBS) - HARP & HIV SNP

Minimum 2 providers of each type:
- Community Psychiatric Support and Treatment (CPST)
- Psychosocial Rehabilitation (PSR)
- Empowerment Services (Peer Supports)
- Family Support and Training
- Habilitation
- Intensive Crisis Respite
- Short Term Crisis Respite
- Education Support Services
- Pre-vocational Services
- Intensive Supported Employment (ISE)
- Ongoing Supported Employment (OSE)
- Transitional Employment

**Urban:** 2 per county | **Rural:** 2 per region

## Urban vs Rural County Classification

**Urban Counties:** Albany, Dutchess, Erie, Kings, Monroe, Nassau, New York, Onondaga, Orange, Orleans, Richmond, Rockland, Saratoga, Schenectady, Suffolk, Westchester

**Rural Counties:** All remaining counties (Allegany, Bronx, Broome, Cattaraugus, Cayuga, Chautauqua, Chemung, Chenango, Clinton, Columbia, Cortland, Delaware, Essex, Franklin, Fulton, Genesee, Greene, Hamilton, Herkimer, Jefferson, Lewis, Livingston, Madison, Montgomery, Niagara, Oneida, Oswego, Otsego, Putnam, Queens, Rensselaer, St. Lawrence, Schoharie, Schuyler, Seneca, Steuben, Sullivan, Tioga, Tompkins, Ulster, Warren, Washington, Wayne, Wyoming, Yates)

**Note:** Bronx and Queens are classified as Urban for RPC regions but have unique requirements due to NYC status.

## Network Adequacy Standards

- **Primary Care:** 10 miles or 30 minutes travel time; appointment within 15 calendar days
- **Specialty Care:** 15 miles or 30 minutes (urban); 30 miles or 60 minutes (rural); appointment within 30 calendar days
- **LTSS:** Varies by service type
- **Emergency Services:** 24/7 access required
