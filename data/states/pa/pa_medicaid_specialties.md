## Network Adequacy Tiers

### Primary Care Providers (PCP) — 2 providers within 30 min urban / 60 min rural
- Primary Care Practitioner (PCP)
  - Definition: "A specific physician, physician group, or CRNP acting within the scope of his or her practice"
  - Includes specialists acting as PCPs (per "Specialists as PCPs" section)

## Notes
- "All Other Provider Types" must meet participant needs through in-network or out-of-network arrangements
- CHC-MCOs should make reasonable efforts to offer at least 2+ Specialty Providers when possible
- If Medicare Network standards require more providers for any type, CHC-MCO must meet Medicare standards
- Network adequacy for some provider types is based on FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) calculations by the Department
