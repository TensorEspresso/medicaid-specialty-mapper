# Ohio Medicaid Managed Care Specialty Reference

## Overview

Ohio Medicaid managed care has **three separate programs** with distinct network adequacy frameworks:

| Program | Population | Specialty Framework | Standards Type |
|---------|------------|---------------------|----------------|
| **MCO** | Adult members + child physical health | 26 specialties (state-defined) | Time/distance by geographic tier |
| **MCOP** (MyCare Ohio) | Dual eligible (Medicaid+Medicare), age 21+ | 28 CMS specialties + 14 facilities | County-based minimums + time/distance |
| **OhioRISE** | Children with serious/complex behavioral health needs | 8 BH specialty types | Distance-only by geographic tier |

**Source documents (Jan 2026 effective):**
- MCO: `2026_01_MCO_Final_1.pdf` — Medicaid Managed Care Organization Provider Agreement
- MCOP: `2026_01_Next_Gen_MyCare_Final_PA.pdf` — Next Generation MyCare Ohio Provider Agreement
- OhioRISE: `2026_01_OhioRISE_Provider_Agreement_Final_Rates_Combined.pdf` — OhioRISE Provider Agreement
- Exception Memo: `2026_MCO_and_MCOP_Exception_Request_Process_Memo.pdf` — Nov 25, 2025

## Geographic Classification

Ohio uses **4 geographic tiers** for time/distance standards:

| Tier | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Large Metro** | Cuyahoga, Franklin, Hamilton, Montgomery counties |
| **Metro** | Other urbanized counties |
| **Micro** | Micropolitan counties |
| **Rural** | Non-metropolitan counties |

Ohio also uses **3 MCO regions** for county-based minimums: West (W), Northeast (NE), Central/Southeast (C/SE).

## Additional MCO Requirements

### Appointment Standards (Table F.1)

| Service Type | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| PCP appointment | 15 calendar days |
| Specialist appointment | 30 calendar days |
| OB/GYN (1st trimester) | 7 calendar days |
| OB/GYN (2nd/3rd trimester) | 3 calendar days |
| Behavioral Health | 7 calendar days |
| CANS assessment (initial) | 72 hours referral → 10 business days completion |

### Pediatric PCP Requirements

To count as a pediatric PCP for time/distance standards:
- Must maintain a general pediatric practice
- Must be listed as pediatrician with Ohio State Medical Board
- Half of required pediatric PCPs must be certified by American Board of Pediatrics

### Hospital Admitting Privileges

If a provider must have hospital admitting privileges to meet credentialing standards, they must have privileges at a hospital under contract with the MCO in the service area to count toward time/distance standards.

### Certified Nurse Midwives

To count toward Gynecology, OB/GYN time/distance standard, must have current hospital privileges at a hospital under contract with the MCO in the service area.

### Out-of-State Providers

MCOs may contract with out-of-state hospitals in bordering states to meet time/distance access requirements.

## Known Issues

- **Oral Surgery standards are outliers**: 98 min / 65 mi (Metro), 110 min / 80 mi (Micro/Rural) — significantly higher than other specialties. This reflects documented recruitment challenges for oral surgery providers in Ohio.
- **No vision/ophthalmology in MCO T&D table**: Vision providers are covered by minimum count requirements (Table F.7) rather than time/distance standards.
- **MCOP time/distance reference ambiguous**: MCOP Table F.2 marks all specialties as "Time/Distance" but the agreement does not include a separate time/distance table for non-LTSS providers. County-based minimums (Table F.6) are the explicit standard. Time/distance for matching specialties assumed to reference MCO Table F.2.
- **Behavioral health complexity**: Ohio uses a multi-layered BH system with OMHAS-certified agencies (types 84, 95), community behavioral health providers, and OhioRISE for children. Provider types have specific enrollment codes.

## NUCC Taxonomy Crosswalk

**Ohio does NOT publish an official NUCC taxonomy crosswalk** for its Medicaid specialties. The MCO agreement Table F.2 lists specialty names without NUCC taxonomy codes or formal definitions.

A crosswalk has been created (`oh_taxonomy_crosswalk.csv`) mapping Ohio specialties to NUCC taxonomy codes using the **current NUCC Health Care Provider Taxonomy Code Set (Version 25.1, July 2025)**. **This is not an official Ohio crosswalk** — it's a best-effort mapping. The NUCC taxonomy has reorganized significantly since the 2017 CMS crosswalk, so specialty-to-code mappings may differ from older references.

| Program | Crosswalk Status |
|---------|-----------------|
| MCO | No official crosswalk. Best-effort mapping in `oh_taxonomy_crosswalk.csv` (26 specialties, 37 taxonomy codes). |
| MCOP | Uses CMS provider panel requirements. Best-effort mapping (27 specialties, 24 taxonomy codes). |
| OhioRISE | No taxonomy crosswalk. Uses Ohio-specific provider type codes (e.g., 37, 42, 47, 52, 84, 95). |

**Key taxonomy changes since 2017:**
- Psychiatry consolidated under `2084A0401X` (Psychiatry & Neurology)
- Clinical Psychology → `103TC0700X` (Psychologist/Clinical)
- Orthopedic Surgery → `207X00000X` (Orthopaedic Surgery)
- Family Practice → `207Q00000X` (Family Medicine)
- Many internal medicine subspecialties use `207R` prefix with specialization codes

### MCOP CMS Provider Panel (Table F.2)

The MCOP uses CMS-defined specialty categories. Key differences from MCO:
- **Primary Care** encompasses general practice, family practice, internal medicine, geriatrics, PCPs, and NPs
- **Oncology** split into Medical/Surgical and Radiation/Radiation Oncology
- Additional specialties: Chiropractor, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Infectious Diseases, Neurosurgery, Ophthalmology, Physiatry/Rehabilitative Medicine, Plastic Surgery, Pulmonology, Rheumatology, Vascular Surgery, Cardiothoracic Surgery, Clinical Psychology
- **Facilities**: Acute Inpatient Hospital Beds, Cardiac Surgery Program, Cardiac Catheterization, ICU, Outpatient/ASC Surgery, Skilled Nursing, Diagnostic Radiology, Mammography, Physical/Occupational/Speech Therapy, Inpatient Psychiatric, Outpatient Infusion/Chemotherapy
