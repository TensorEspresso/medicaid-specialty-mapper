# Michigan Medicaid Managed Care Network Adequacy — Specialty Standards Research

## 1. State Agency and Program Name

- **State Agency**: Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS)
- **Program**: Medicaid Managed Care — Comprehensive Health Care Program (CHCP)
- **Regulatory Oversight**: 
  - MDHHS administers Medicaid program and establishes network adequacy standards
  - Michigan Department of Insurance and Financial Services (DIFS) performs initial review and licensure for HMOs, but MDHHS issues final approvals
- **Managed Care Entities**: 
  - MCOs (Managed Care Organizations) for physical health
  - PIHPs (Primary Integrated Health Plans) for specialty behavioral health services
- **Service Area Structure**: 10 state-designated "Prosperity Regions" (each served by one or more health plans)

## 2. County/Tier Structure

Michigan uses **5 CMS county type designations** applied across all 83 counties:

| Tier | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Large Metro** | Population ≥1M & density ≥1,000/sq mi; OR population 500K-999K & density ≥1,500/sq mi; OR any population & density ≥5,000/sq mi |
| **Metro** | Various population/density combinations (e.g., 1M+ population & 10-999.9 density/sq mi) |
| **Micro** | Population 50K-199K & density 10-99.9/sq mi; OR population 10K-49K & density 50-999.9/sq mi |
| **Rural** | Population 10K-49K & density 10-49.9/sq mi; OR population <10K & density 50-999.9/sq mi |
| **CEAC** (Counties with Extreme Access Considerations) | Any population size with density <10 people/sq mi |

**Access Compliance Thresholds:**
- Large Metro & Metro counties: ≥90% of beneficiaries must have access within time/distance standards
- Micro, Rural, CEAC counties: ≥85% of beneficiaries must have access within time/distance standards
- Both time AND distance must be met simultaneously

## 4. Time/Distance and Caseload Requirements

### Time/Distance Standards (Physical Health)
- Measured in **minutes** (travel time) and **miles** (distance)
- Both time AND distance must be met
- Minimum of **2 providers** required within time/distance standards in Large Metro and Metro counties (for Primary Care, Hospital, OB/GYN)

### Provider-to-Enrollee Ratio Standards (Physical Health)
- **Primary Care – Adult**: 1:500 (except Kalkaska 1:692, Missaukee 1:873, Schoolcraft 1:806; all other counties 1:650)
- **Primary Care – Pediatric**: 1:500
- **General Dentistry**: 1:650 (except Kalkaska 1:692, Missaukee 1:873, Schoolcraft 1:806)
- Providers must be enrolled in Medicaid and work ≥20 hours/week per practice location to count

### Timely Access Standards (Physical Health)
| Type of Care | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| Emergency Services | Immediately, 24/7 |
| Urgent Care | Within 48 hours |
| Routine Care | Within 30 business days of request |
| Non-Urgent Symptomatic Care | Within 7 business days of request |
| Specialty Care | Within 6 weeks of request |
| Acute Specialty Care | Within 5 business days of request |
| Behavioral Health | Non-life-threatening emergency within 6 hours; Urgent within 48 hours; Routine within 10 business days |
| Prenatal Care – Initial | 1st/2nd trimester: within 7 business days; 3rd trimester: within 3 business days; High risk: within 3 business days |

### Dental Timely Access Standards
| Type of Care | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| Emergency Dental | Immediately, 24/7 |
| Urgent Dental | Within 48 hours |
| Routine Dental | Within 21 business days |
| Preventive Dental | Within 6 weeks |
| Initial Dental Appointment | Within 8 weeks |

## 6. Taxonomy Crosswalk

**The `mi_taxonomy_crosswalk.csv` is a best-effort semantic mapping of MI DIFS/MDHHS specialty categories to NUCC v25.1 taxonomy codes.** All `match_confidence` values are marked `best-effort`.

- **DIFS** lists 51 individual provider specialty types with internal codes but does NOT publish NUCC taxonomy codes.
- **BCBSM** (`sources/bcbsm_taxonomy.txt`) provides NUCC code mappings for a subset of MI specialties — used as a ground-truth reference where available.
- Where BCBSM does not cover a specialty (e.g., Endocrinology, Rheumatology, Vascular Surgery), the NUCC code was selected from the authoritative NUCC v25.1 taxonomy based on semantic matching of the specialty name.
- 14 hallucinated codes were identified and corrected against NUCC v25.1 (e.g., `207RG1001X` → `207RG0300X` for Geriatrics).
- 15 rows with empty `nucc_code` correspond to facility types (hospitals, SNFs, ASCs) — provider taxonomy does not apply.
