## Name: "Tensor Automata"

**Claim:** Technical jargon names create expectation gaps for service businesses.

**Status:** VALIDATED

- Stripe's naming framework and branding research confirm: technical jargon names (Tensor, Neural, Cortex) signal "technology company" rather than "problem solver"
- Non-technical buyers may not know what "tensor" means — creates alienation gap
- Successful AI *service* names tend to be abstract/invented (Anthropic, Cohere) or outcome-oriented (Observe.AI, Scale AI)
- Pure technical jargon works better for infrastructure products than service businesses
- **Caveat:** Still distinctive and trademarkable. The gap closes as you grow into the name.

**Sources:** Stripe "How to pick a name for your startup: 11 steps" (2023), Dr. Dana Loewy on technical jargon in business communication

## Service-First, Then Productize

**Claim:** Start as a service to learn client problems, then productize repeatable deliverables.

**Status:** VALIDATED

**Proven examples:**
- Scale AI: started doing data labeling → built tools internally → productized ($7B+ valuation)
- Cognigy (Düsseldorf): chatbot services → platform
- DesignJoy: $4,995/month unlimited design, 1 person, $3.1M ARR (2024)
- KlientBoost: SEO → $1.5M ARR as productized service

**Post-mortem evidence:**
- Kristijan Arapov (arapovk.com, Aug 2025): shut down $24K/month AI agency at peak — pure service without productization led to burnout and unsustainable scope
- Fix: fixed-scope, fixed-price packages

**AI-specific productized service examples:**
- AI Readiness Audit: $500-$2,000
- Workflow Automation Setup: $2,000 flat fee
- LLM Integration Package: $5K-$25K
- AI Agent Development: $10K-$50K
- RAG Implementation: $5K-$30K

**Pricing data:**
- Setup/implementation: $2,000-$5,000
- Monthly retainer: $1,500-$5,000/month
- Don't price below $1,500/month — low-priced clients take same work as high-priced ones
- Move away from hourly billing entirely

**Sources:** Justin McKelvey (justinmckelvey.com, Apr 2026), Jatin Bhutani (jatinbhutani.com), getaiform.com case study ($12K/month productized AI audit), Medium (Anuj Bhalla, $47K in 90 days), Reddit r/AiAutomations pricing discussions (Apr 2026)

## When to Register Domain and Incorporate

**Claim:** Wait until you have revenue to incorporate; register domain when you need it.

**Status:** PARTIALLY VALIDATED / CORRECTED

**Domains:** Register early. $10-15/year is cheap insurance against losing the name. No data supports "waiting" for domains specifically.

**Incorporation:** The "wait until revenue" advice is anecdotal, not data-backed. Practical trigger: incorporate when you have a contract or are about to take money from a client. Not a revenue threshold — a legal one.

- Sole proprietor is fine for validation phase
- LLC formation: $90-$1,250 depending on state
- YC pushes founders to incorporate early (Delaware C-Corp) for investor readiness
- Stripe recommends incorporating before taking payments

**Sources:** Stripe incorporation guidance, Y Combinator startup guidance, Reddit r/Entrepreneur and r/llc_life discussions, Forbes/Entrepreneur/SBA incorporation advice

## Local/On-Prem Inference as Differentiator

**Claim:** Local inference is a real differentiator for healthcare and enterprise AI services.

**Status:** STRONGLY VALIDATED

**Companies explicitly positioning on-prem as core value prop:**
- Giga ML (YC-backed, $3.6M raised) — secure, offline LLM deployment for enterprises
- Cohere — private deployments via VPC/on-prem, partnered with Dell for enterprise search
- AgenixHub — "Sovereign AI" for healthcare systems with 200+ beds and strict HIPAA
- Trillet — on-prem hosting + configurable data residency for healthcare/finance

**Industry analysis:**
- Forbes Tech Council (Oct 2025): "Smart AI startups are building on-premise from day one" — competitive advantage, not cost optimization
- Dev.to: "Compliance requirements often mandate on-premise deployment regardless of volume. Private LLM deployment becomes a functional requirement."
- LinkedIn: "Healthcare organizations globally are discovering that local LLM deployment is the only path to using AI on patient data without violating regulations."

**Healthcare buyer requirements (non-negotiable):**
- HIPAA compliance (avg breach cost: $10.9M in 2025 — highest of any industry for 14 consecutive years)
- Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) — mandatory before any vendor touches PHI
- Data residency — geographic boundaries, state laws, payer contracts
- Audit trails and de-identification capabilities
- Real example: NYC hospitals dropped an AI firm over data privacy concerns

**Sources:** Forbes Tech Council (Oct 2025), Giga ML website/funding, AgenixHub, Trillet, Vantage.io vendor security questionnaires, LinkedIn (Samuel Morhaim, AI-PHI specialist)

## "Sell Outcomes, Not Technology"

**Claim:** Outcome-based positioning is more effective than technology-based positioning for AI service businesses.

**Status:** STRONGLY VALIDATED

**Industry research:**
- Bessemer Venture Partners (Feb 2026 AI Pricing Playbook): AI companies should "price for outcomes, not access." Predicts 70% of SaaS vendors will abandon per-seat pricing by 2028. Per-seat already dropped from 21% to 15% in 12 months.
- Forbes Agency Council: "Sell Outcomes, Not Hours — billable hours won't survive the AI era"
- Pax8 (May 2026): "Sell agentic AI by pricing outcomes and proving ROI"
- BCG research (2021): "Companies are shifting from selling products and services to providing outcomes"

**Practitioner evidence:**
- MSP Today: "Package AI technology in a manner that is understandable, easy to implement, and aligned with SMB business processes"
- Henry Earle-A'Hern (Substack): "The agencies that win won't sell deliverables. They'll sell results."
- Sierra AI: "Outcome-based pricing for AI Agents — pay only when the software achieves specific outcomes"
- Medium (52-year-old non-tech founder): "Business owners paid for 'more customers,' not 'AI-generated copy'"

**Sources:** Bessemer Venture Partners AI Pricing Playbook (Feb 2026), Forbes Agency Council, Pax8 (May 2026), BCG research, Sierra AI, Zendesk (Apr 2026)
